# Decision points

Use one decision question at the stage where its answer changes the work. Present
the unresolved question plainly and wait; do not answer it for the participants.

## Product prioritization

Question: Which Projects matter enough to shape now?

Output: an ordered set of goals, not premature implementation cards.

## Product refinement

Question: Which Discovery questions must be answered inside each Project?

Output: owned, time-boxed Discovery work with specified decision artifacts.

## Technical refinement

Question: Given what Discovery established, what complete MiniProjects and Chores
make up the Project?

Output: delivery bundles that are complete, demoable, owned, and launch-ready,
plus any work that must return to Discovery.

## Technical estimation

Question: What effort belongs to each Chore and complete MiniProject?

Output: estimates for delivery work and time boxes for Discovery. Do not estimate
technical-layer fragments independently and then call their sum a MiniProject.

## Sprint selection

Question: Which ready work best serves the current business priority?

Output: selected work from the Chore, Discovery, and MiniProject lanes without
changing the classification merely to fit a sprint.
