# Tech Refinement

Tech Refinement turns Discovery output into MiniProjects one engineer can own end
to end.

Work through these four questions **one at a time**, in order:

1. What is complete and demoable?
2. What can one engineer own end to end?
3. What can be tested and made launch-ready?
4. What needs more Discovery first?

## How to run it

Ask question 1. Then stop and wait for the answer.

Do not ask question 2 until the user has answered question 1. Do not answer any of
these questions yourself, and do not guess the user's answer in order to keep going.
The answers are the user's to give — they carry context about priority, urgency, and
customer impact that is not in the repository.

If you do not have an answer, stop and ask. Ending your turn with an unanswered
question is correct behaviour here, not a failure.
